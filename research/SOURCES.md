# Sources & Bibliography

Consolidated bibliography for music-psychology and state-change research referenced across this hub, vibe-os docs, and agent Expertise sections. APA 7 format. Entries are linked to the open questions or agents they inform.

## Tempo Entrainment (→ MQ-001)

Lacey, J. I., & Lacey, B. C. (1970). Some autonomic-central nervous system interrelationships. *Physiological Correlates of Emotion*, 205–227.

Thaut, M. H., McIntosh, G. C., & Rice, R. R. (1997). Rhythmic facilitation of gait training in hemiparetic stroke rehabilitation. *Journal of the Neurological Sciences*, 151(2), 207–212. https://doi.org/10.1016/S0022-510X(97)00146-9

Nozaradan, S., Peretz, I., Missal, M., & Mouraux, A. (2011). Tagging the neuronal entrainment to beat and meter. *Journal of Neuroscience*, 31(28), 10234–10240. https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0765-11.2011

Wieczorkowska, A., Kubik, A., & Brzozowski, M. (2023). Music tempo and heart rate — a systematic review. *Frontiers in Psychology* (meta-analytic summary; consult directly for full citation).

## Mode and Valence (→ MQ-002)

Hevner, K. (1935). The affective character of the major and minor modes in music. *American Journal of Psychology*, 47(1), 103–118. https://doi.org/10.2307/1416710

Krumhansl, C. L. (1990). *Cognitive foundations of musical pitch*. Oxford University Press.

Fritz, T., Jentschke, S., Gosselin, N., Sammler, D., Peretz, I., Turner, R., Friederici, A. D., & Koelsch, S. (2009). Universal recognition of three basic emotions in music. *Current Biology*, 19(7), 573–576. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2009.02.058

Sievers, B., Polansky, L., Casey, M., & Wheatley, T. (2013). Music and movement share a dynamic structure that supports universal expressions of emotion. *PNAS*, 110(1), 70–75. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1209023110

## ISO Principle (→ MQ-003)

Altshuler, I. M. (1948). A psychiatrist's experience with music as a therapeutic agent. In D. Schullian & M. Schoen (Eds.), *Music and Medicine*. Henry Schuman.

Bruscia, K. E. (2014). *Defining music therapy* (3rd ed.). Barcelona Publishers.

Thaut, M. H., & Hoemberg, V. (Eds.). (2014). *Handbook of neurologic music therapy*. Oxford University Press.

## Binaural Beats (→ MQ-004)

Oster, G. (1973). Auditory beats in the brain. *Scientific American*, 229(4), 94–102.

Garcia-Argibay, M., Santed, M. A., & Reales, J. M. (2019). Efficacy of binaural auditory beats in cognition, anxiety, and pain perception: A meta-analysis. *Psychological Research*, 83(2), 357–372. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00423-018-01060-0

Becher, A. K., Höhne, M., Axmacher, N., Chaieb, L., Elger, C. E., & Fell, J. (2015). Intracranial electroencephalography power and phase synchronization changes during monaural and binaural beat stimulation. *European Journal of Neuroscience*, 41(2), 254–263.

## Lyric Psychology and Self-Affirmation (→ MQ-005)

Cohen, G. L., & Sherman, D. K. (2014). The psychology of change: Self-affirmation and social psychological intervention. *Annual Review of Psychology*, 65, 333–371. https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-010213-115137

Northoff, G., Heinzel, A., de Greck, M., Bermpohl, F., Dobrowolny, H., & Panksepp, J. (2006). Self-referential processing in our brain — A meta-analysis of imaging studies on the self. *NeuroImage*, 31(1), 440–457.

Chanda, M. L., & Levitin, D. J. (2013). The neurochemistry of music. *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*, 17(4), 179–193. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2013.02.007
